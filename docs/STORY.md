# Kaleidoscope — story summary (for collaborators)

The full, living story bible is a Claude Doc owned by Chris ("Kaleidoscope — Story Bible"). This file is the
decided summary. If they disagree, the bible wins; ask Chris.

## Decisions (all final)
- **Chapter 1 title:** *The Sundown Batch*.
- **Premise:** Saturday Aug 19 1967, 4:00 PM. Ex-SFPD private eye Frank Tolliver wakes in a Haight-Ashbury attic
  missing three days, his .38 and the girl he was hired to find (Rain Whitcombe, 19). By 7:30 PM, Dr. Ambrose Kell
  (Lotus Foundation, government-funded via "Mr. Garrison") will hand out 5,000 free tabs of **LETHE** at the
  Panhandle gathering: a drug that erases the last few months and leaves people calm, pleasant and compliant.
  It doesn't kill. Frank got a 3-day dose on Thursday, which makes him the proof that it works.
- **Rain** has had a full dose: she's "Sister Rain," pouring tea in the Foundation parlor, and doesn't know Frank.
- **Four impossible things** (unexplained, treated as normal):
  1. **The kaleidoscope** (Rain's childhood toy). USE it on the right thing and a memory shard returns.
     USE or GIVE it to Rain and she remembers who she is.
  2. **Phones ring for Frank**, and the caller is Frank himself, from Thursday. This is the hint system
     (10 min each, counted on the ending card).
  3. **The pigeon** (one bent tail feather) turns up everywhere and watches. It's a running gag in the death texts.
  4. **Moondog's posters** show things that haven't happened yet.
- **Voice:** second person, present tense. Murakami, Adams and Brautigan lead: weird and absurd, but never too weird.
  Pynchon only in the conspiracy's paperwork; noir is the skeleton the jokes hang on. At most one small odd
  thing per scene, always treated as normal. Every LOOK earns its click. The deaths carry the biggest comedy.
  Reference lines are in `src/chapters/ch1` (the attic intro, the toast, the ginger-ale death, Kell on the stairs).
- **Endings:** Best (batch burned + Rain herself + ledger + all 5 shards; the pigeon delivers a *Fellowship*
  envelope from Laguna Beach), Good (a shard missing), Bittersweet (no ledger), Lonely (cellar route, Rain never
  reached), plus the 7:30 clock as a death.
- **Originality:** no real people, bands, songs or groups; no text, characters or catchphrases from the four
  authors. Influences only.
